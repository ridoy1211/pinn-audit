## Preparation requirements for the provisional candidate

The [SN Computer Science author instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/42979/submission-guidelines)
ask for a structured 150–250-word abstract with Purpose, Methods, Results and
Conclusion, and 4–6 keywords. They identify Word or LaTeX source formats and
numbered citations. Our Markdown body is a drafting source, not the final
submission format. The treatment of cited preprints should be checked against
the final policy and editorial requirements rather than silently relabelling
Jekic's preprint as a journal article.

The same instructions require author information and relevant declarations.
They distinguish substantive LLM assistance from copy editing; our workflow
included substantive assistance in coding, analysis and drafting. An accurate
account is needed, without claiming human checks that have not occurred.
